# Report 141: C benchmark at `4a49d0dfe`, after #341–#347 (2026-11-27)

## What was measured

The same frozen benchmark, compilers and commands as
[report 140](2026-11-26-140-LLVM-Z80-Benchmark.md), with MinZ moved from `7d1119456` to
`4a49d0dfe`. SDCC 4.6.0 #16555 and llvm-z80 clang 23.1.5 (release 23.1.5-r1) are
unchanged, and their rows are identical to report 140. All 272 rows pass the result,
memory and PC/SP checks. Data: [`reports/c89-benchmark-2026-11-27-llvm/`](c89-benchmark-2026-11-27-llvm/).

## What changed in MinZ between the two runs

| Change | Case it was aimed at | Before | After |

| Address reassociation, byte-pack lanes, direct call-argument addresses (#346) | fat12_decode | 24384 T, 148 B | 13952 T, 122 B |

"Before" is left empty where the earlier value was not re-measured for this report.
#338, #339, #341, #345 and #347 are correctness fixes and were not aimed at speed.

## Reading

- MinZ default against llvm-z80 -O2: 0.764 of its T-states (0.874 in report 140) at
  0.914 of its size (0.939). Against SDCC speed: 0.550 (0.629).
- llvm-z80 -O2 is faster on 6 cases (8 in report 140). `fat12_decode` and
  `fusion_map_filter_sum` moved to the MinZ side. The largest remaining gap is
  `in_interval` (1.35), then `fusion_window_sum` (1.15); the other four are within 8%.
- `fusion_dot_product` is still 1638 bytes against 633: the default profile uses the
  quarter-square multiply table.

## Limits

The 26 cases are our own frozen set, and several changes in this period were made while
looking at individual cases of it (crc8, popcount8, fat12_decode, fusion_map_store). Each
rule is stated on IR facts and has its own tests, but the geometric mean over this set is
no longer an estimate for unseen programs. An unseen set has not been frozen yet. One
llvm-z80 release and two flag sets were measured; programs are leaf kernels without libc,
I/O or startup code.
